About Me

I am a Cyber Security graduate with a strong passion for **defensive security** and building resilient systems. I enjoy working on the Blue Team side of security—monitoring environments, detecting threats early, and helping organisations respond to incidents in a structured and confident way.

My interests revolve around SOC operations, SIEM monitoring, log analysis, and threat detection. I like diving into logs, correlating events, and understanding how small signals can reveal bigger attacks. I constantly explore new tools, frameworks, and methodologies to improve detection and response capabilities.

Beyond technical skills, I value clear communication, documentation, and teamwork. I enjoy collaborating with engineers, analysts, and stakeholders to translate complex security findings into practical actions that actually reduce risk.

Vision

My vision is to become a trusted Blue Team defender who helps organisations build proactive, data‑driven security operations. I aim to contribute to security teams that do more than react—they anticipate, prepare, and continuously improve. I want to use my skills in monitoring, analysis, and collaboration to create safer digital environments for users, businesses, and communities.
